IELTS ACADEMIC WRITING TASK 1: THE MASTER GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------
To score a Band 7.0 or higher in Task 1, you must demonstrate strong vocabulary, perfect paragraph structure, and an ability to summarize data without getting lost in every single number. 

Here is the exact, step-by-step method you must use for every single chart, graph, or map.

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: THE INTRODUCTION (1 Sentence Only)
Your only job here is to tell the examiner what the chart is about. You do this by paraphrasing the prompt.

* Original Prompt: "The graph below shows the consumption of fish and some different kinds of meat in a European country between 1979 and 2004."
* Your Paraphrase: "The provided line graph illustrates the amount of fish and various types of meat consumed in a European nation over a 25-year period from 1979 to 2004."

Vocabulary to memorize for Introductions:
- shows -> illustrates, demonstrates, compares, highlights
- the number of -> the quantity of, the figure for, the proportion of (if percentages)
- between 1990 and 2000 -> over a ten-year period from 1990 to 2000

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: THE OVERVIEW (1 to 2 Sentences)
**THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT PARAGRAPH IN TASK 1.** 
If you do not write a clear overview, you cannot score higher than a Band 5.0 in Task Achievement. 

Rule: Do NOT write any numbers or percentages here. Just tell me the biggest, most obvious trends.
- For a Line Graph: What went up the most? What went down? What was the highest overall?
- For a Bar Chart: Which bar was the tallest? Which was the shortest? Did anything change over time?
- For a Map: What was the biggest change? (e.g., "Overall, the town became much more modernized and residential, with many green spaces being replaced by housing.")

Example of a perfect Overview:
"Overall, it is immediately apparent that the consumption of chicken increased significantly over the period, while beef and lamb experienced a downward trend. Fish remained the least popular food item throughout the 25 years."

--------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 & 4: THE BODY PARAGRAPHS (Details and Numbers)
Here, you must prove the statements you made in your Overview using the specific numbers from the chart. You must divide the data logically into two paragraphs.

How to group data:
- By Time: Paragraph 3 (1990 to 2000), Paragraph 4 (2000 to 2010).
- By Trend: Paragraph 3 (Things that went up), Paragraph 4 (Things that went down).
- By Category: Paragraph 3 (Men's data), Paragraph 4 (Women's data).

VOCABULARY FOR TRENDS (Line Graphs / Bar Charts with time)

1. Upward Trends:
- Verbs: increased, rose, grew, climbed, surged, skyrocketed, peaked at
- Nouns: an increase, a rise, a growth, an upward trend
- Example: "The number of visitors surged to 5 million in 2010."

2. Downward Trends:
- Verbs: decreased, fell, dropped, declined, plummeted, plunged, hit a low of
- Nouns: a decrease, a fall, a drop, a downward trend
- Example: "Car sales plummeted from 50,000 to just 10,000."

3. Stable Trends:
- Verbs: remained stable, remained constant, plateaued, leveled off
- Example: "The percentage of women in the workforce remained stable at 45%."

4. Fluctuations:
- Verbs: fluctuated, varied
- Example: "The price of oil fluctuated wildly between $40 and $80."

ADVERBS & ADJECTIVES (To show how fast or slow a change was)
- Big changes: significantly, dramatically, sharply, drastically, rapidly
- Small changes: slightly, gradually, steadily, marginally
- Example: "The population increased gradually before rising sharply in 1990."

--------------------------------------------------
CRITICAL GRAMMAR RULES FOR TASK 1:

1. Check the Date! 
If the chart says 1990, you MUST use the Past Simple tense (increased, fell). 
If the chart shows predictions for 2050, use future tenses (is expected to increase, is predicted to fall).

2. Passive Voice for Process Diagrams!
If you are describing how coffee is made, do NOT write "The man picks the beans." 
You must write: "First, the beans are picked by hand. Following this, they are dried in the sun."
