# IELTS Academic Writing Task 1: The Fundamentals

Welcome to your first lesson! For Academic Task 1, you must write a report based on visual information (a chart, graph, table, map, or process diagram). 

## Core Requirements
*   **Word Count:** You must write **at least 150 words** (aim for 160-180 words). Writing less than 150 words will lower your score.
*   **Time Limit:** You should spend about **20 minutes** on this task.
*   **Objective:** Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
*   **Tone:** Academic, objective, and formal. **Never** give your personal opinion or explain *why* the data looks the way it does. Just report what you see.

## The Perfect 4-Paragraph Structure

To get a high score, you should always structure your response into these four paragraphs:

### Paragraph 1: The Introduction (1 sentence)
You simply paraphrase (rewrite in your own words) the prompt given to you in the exam.
*   *Prompt says:* "The chart below shows the number of men and women studying engineering at Australian universities."
*   *You write:* "The provided bar chart illustrates the gender distribution of engineering students across universities in Australia."

### Paragraph 2: The Overview (1-2 sentences) -> **CRITICAL FOR A HIGH SCORE**
You must highlight the 2 or 3 most obvious, major trends or differences. Do NOT use any specific numbers or percentages here. 
*   *Example:* "Overall, it is clear that significantly more men than women studied engineering over the period. However, while the number of male students fluctuated, the number of female students experienced a steady increase."

### Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 1 (Details)
Group the data logically. Here, you start giving the specific data (numbers, dates, percentages) to support the main trends you mentioned in the overview. 

### Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 2 (Details)
Continue grouping the remaining data logically. Compare the data against each other where appropriate.
