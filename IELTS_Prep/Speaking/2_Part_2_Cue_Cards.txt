IELTS Speaking Practice
Section: Part 2 (The Cue Card)

INSTRUCTIONS:
In the exam, you will be given a topic to talk about for 1 to 2 minutes. You have 1 minute to make notes before you speak.
Take a minute to think, then type out what you would say for 2 minutes (about 150-200 words).

CUE CARD TOPIC:
Describe a time when you helped someone.
You should say:
- Who you helped
- How you helped them
- Where it happened
And explain how you felt about helping them.
